# AfterCosts — FAQ

## Is this a Shopify App Store app?
No. AfterCosts reads Shopify CSV exports locally in your browser. It does not connect with the Shopify API.

## Are customer CSVs uploaded?
No. The tool processes them in the browser. If you opt into the separate contact form, your contact details are submitted to AfterCosts.

## Is it free?
Yes. The free browser evaluation includes the calculation workflow. An optional $29 one-time offline toolkit includes portable HTML and worksheets with overlapping capabilities.

## Is this exact accounting profit?
No. Results are management estimates, depending on your source data and assumptions. They are not audited financial reports or tax advice.

## Why do deposits differ from contribution?
Settlement dates, taxes, returns, disputes, reserves and processor adjustments may differ. AfterCosts does not reconcile bank payouts.

## Can I post merchant CSVs in GitHub Issues?
Please don't. Use fictional test data and never post customer information, credentials or paid links.
